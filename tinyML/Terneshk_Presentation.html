Todays Solutions

Cloud IDS, while suitable for some application, has the following drawbacks

  • High Latency
  • Privacy risk
  • Dependence on continuous connectivity

Todays Solutions - continue

Local Server, could be a feasible solution for some but has the folowing cons

  • Increases system complexity
  • Too costly for large-scale or low-margin applications (e.g. Traffic cameras)
5 / 15

Todays Solutions - continue

Deploying IDS directly on IoT/IIoT devices can address many challenges.

  • Rule-based IDS cannot keep up with AI-driven and evolving cyberattacks.
  • IoT devices lack sufficient compute power & memory to run traditional AI-based IDS.
6 / 15

Our Proposed Solution

TinyML Intrusion Detection SDK for MCUs.

An AI based IDS:

  • Small enough to fit into embedded devices (less than 1MB)
  • Fast response: < 300 µs latency
  • High accuracy: >98 % accuracy
  • Plug-and-play C/C++ library.
  • Covers detection requirement requested by IEC 62443.

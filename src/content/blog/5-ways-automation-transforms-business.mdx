Cloud-heavy security leaves gaps for industrial systems. Here are five reasons to push intrusion detection onto the device itself.

1) **Latency and Safety**  
   Controllers can’t wait on cloud round-trips. Local inference keeps alerts within control-loop budgets (<10 ms targets).

2) **Privacy and Compliance**  
   Sensitive telemetry stays on the device, reducing exposure and easing CRA/NIS2 evidence gathering.

3) **Resilience When Offline**  
   Connectivity drops shouldn’t blind security. On-device IDS keeps watching, then syncs evidence when links return.

4) **Footprint-Fit for MCUs**  
   TinySentinel models fit <256 KB and respect strict RAM/energy budgets—no extra hardware required.

5) **Operational Trust**  
   OT teams get deterministic behavior, tunable alerts, and runbooks that match how plants actually operate.

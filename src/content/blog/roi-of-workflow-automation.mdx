When device fleets are scattered across factories, energy sites, or remote assets, security must live on the device itself. TinySentinel is purpose-built for microcontroller-class hardware, but how do you quantify its impact? This guide outlines the ROI levers for on-device intrusion detection.

## What Changes with On-Device IDS

- **Latency:** Detection happens locally (<10 ms) instead of waiting on gateways or cloud round-trips.
- **Footprint:** Models fit within <256 KB and respect tight RAM/energy budgets.
- **Privacy:** Sensitive telemetry stays on the device, reducing exposure and compliance overhead.
- **Resilience:** Alerts continue during connectivity gaps; evidence syncs when links return.

## Calculating the Impact

1) **Manual Monitoring Avoided**  
   Replace periodic log reviews and remote diagnostics with continuous on-device scoring. Estimate hours avoided per device × operator rate × working days.

2) **Telemetry Reduction**  
   Less raw data leaves the device. Factor reduced bandwidth/ingest fees and lower cloud processing.

3) **Downtime Avoidance**  
   Faster detection means fewer unplanned stoppages. Use typical outage cost per hour × expected incidents prevented.

4) **Compliance Readiness**  
   On-device analysis supports CRA/NIS2 expectations. Model the cost/time saved in evidence collection and audits.

## Example: Single Line Pilot

- Devices: 40 controllers  
- Manual checks avoided: 1 hour/day per device  
- Operator rate: $60/hour  
- Integration cost: $15,000  

**Annual savings:** 40 × 1 × 250 × $60 = **$600,000**  
**Break-even:** Integration recovered in **~1 month**  
**Bonus:** Bandwidth drop of ~30% by keeping telemetry local.

## What to Measure in Your Pilot

- Inference latency vs. control loop budget  
- Model footprint vs. flash/RAM budget  
- Alert precision/recall on attack simulations  
- Energy overhead compared to baseline  
- Operator workflow fit (runbooks, escalation paths)

## Building Your Business Case

1. Start with one device class and a clear threat hypothesis.  
2. Define success thresholds (latency, footprint, accuracy, offline behavior).  
3. Capture before/after metrics for bandwidth, time-to-detect, and operator effort.  
4. Package evidence for stakeholders and compliance teams.

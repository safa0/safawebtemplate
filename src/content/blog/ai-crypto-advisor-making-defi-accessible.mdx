## How It Works (In Plain English)

Imagine you're a shop owner who needs to decide when to restock your shelves. You could guess, or you could use data about past sales, upcoming events, and current inventory to make a smart decision.

Our AI does something similar for crypto liquidity:

### 1. It Learns From the Past

We analyze 45 days of price history to understand how a crypto pair typically behaves. This includes patterns like daily volatility cycles, weekend behavior, and responses to market events.

### 2. It Predicts the Future

Using advanced forecasting models, we predict where prices are likely to go in the next 6-12 hours. But we don't just give you a single number—we provide confidence intervals so you know how certain we are.

### 3. It Considers the Costs

Every trade has fees. Gas costs on Ethereum can be significant. Our system only recommends action when the benefits clearly outweigh the costs.

### 4. It Knows When to Wait

This is the key insight: sometimes the smartest move is no move at all. If prices are likely to return to your range, we'll tell you to hold steady rather than lock in losses by rebalancing prematurely.

## The Problem We Solved: Impermanent Loss

One of the biggest challenges for liquidity providers is "impermanent loss"—the difference between holding assets in a liquidity pool versus simply holding them in your wallet.

When prices move and you rebalance, you lock in this loss. But here's the key insight our AI uses: if prices are likely to come back, waiting can save you money.

Our system calculates the probability of price returning to your range and recommends waiting when it makes financial sense. This single feature has helped users avoid unnecessary losses from premature rebalancing.
